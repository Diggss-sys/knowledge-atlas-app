# Team run — be a participant, send us 3 files (~5 minutes)

*We're testing whether the room study runs well on everyone's machine before we go further. You don't
need Unity, git, or any setup — just download an app, do one short session as a participant, and send
back the files it saves. This is also our first study dry-run, so do it honestly.*

## 1. Get the app

- **Windows:** download **[KnowledgeAtlasStudy-Windows.zip](https://github.com/Diggss-sys/knowledge-atlas-app/releases/download/team-run-2026-07-10/KnowledgeAtlasStudy-Windows.zip)**
  (from the [team-run-2026-07-10 release](https://github.com/Diggss-sys/knowledge-atlas-app/releases/tag/team-run-2026-07-10)),
  then right-click → **Extract All**. Open the folder and double-click **KnowledgeAtlasStudy.exe**.
  - Windows may warn "Windows protected your PC" (unsigned app). Click **More info → Run anyway**.
- **Mac:** get `KnowledgeAtlasStudy.app` from **Michael** (Apple won't let us hand out an unsigned Mac
  app over the web cleanly). First launch: **right-click the app → Open → Open** (needed once because
  it's unsigned).

The app opens in a window. If it opens fullscreen and you want a window, that's fine either way.

## 2. Do one session (about 3–4 minutes)

1. **Participant ID:** type anything memorable — your initials + a number is perfect (e.g. `MJ7`).
   Click **Begin**.
2. **Instructions → Start.**
3. For each of the 4 rooms:
   - Click **Enter room**.
   - **Walk around** with **W A S D**, look with the **mouse**. Actually look up at the ceiling and
     around the space — take 15–30 seconds, don't rush.
   - Press **Esc** to leave.
   - Answer the one question (**1–7**). There are no right answers — your honest impression.
4. **Session complete.**

## 3. Send us your files

On the final screen, click **Open results folder**. You'll see three files that start with
`response-` (a `.csv`, a `.jsonl`, and a `.perf.csv`). **Send all three** in the team chat, and tell us:

- which machine you used (e.g. "M2 MacBook Air", "gaming PC with RTX 3070", "2019 Intel MacBook").

That's it. You're done.

## What we're actually checking

The `.perf.csv` quietly recorded the frame rate while you walked each room — this tells us whether the
app runs smoothly on your hardware (especially the Macs) and, importantly, whether it runs *equally*
smoothly in every room. That matters for the science, not just comfort. You never saw a frame counter
on purpose — an on-screen number would distract a real participant.

## If something goes wrong

- **Black screen / nothing renders:** note your machine and tell us — that's exactly the kind of thing
  this run exists to catch. Don't force it.
- **Can't move in a room:** click inside the window first so it has focus, then W A S D.
- **Stuck in a room:** press **Esc**.
- **"Where are my files?":** the **Open results folder** button on the last screen opens them for you.
  (For reference they live under the app's save folder: Windows
  `%USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\COGS 160 Research Lab\Room Studio\`, Mac
  `~/Library/Application Support/COGS 160 Research Lab/Room Studio/`.)

Thanks — this five minutes from each of you is what turns "it works on Paco's PC" into "it works on the
machines we'll actually run the study on."
